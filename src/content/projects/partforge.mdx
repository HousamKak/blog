An agent can write OpenSCAD, but a nice render tells you nothing about whether the part will print, fit or hold. Partforge closes that gap. It is one core with two front ends, a terminal command (`pf`) and an MCP server, spanning OpenSCAD, headless Bambu Studio slicing, Bambu printers and SolidWorks.

`pf build` renders, measures, judges printability and slices in one pass, and comes back with grams and print time. The checks are real metrology: watertightness, wall-thickness distribution, overhang area, tipping risk. Fits come from the ISO 286 tables, and the `.scad` library has real ISO metric threads and print-aware holes.

On the SolidWorks side it drives the app over COM, and an add-in task pane sends whatever I have selected straight to a Claude session.

**Stack:** Node.js, OpenSCAD, Bambu Studio CLI, SolidWorks COM API, C#, Python, MCP.
