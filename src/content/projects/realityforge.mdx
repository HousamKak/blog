Game physics usually starts from Newton and bolts on tweaks. I wanted to go the other way: declare what a world is made of and which principles must hold, and derive the family of physical laws that are allowed. Then run them as executable theories and see what happens.

RealityForge is a research engine that does that. A construction compiler works over finite groups and graphs, and an independent verifier checks what it produces, so a world cannot claim a symmetry it does not have. Before a world runs, the engine classifies its energy and stability exactly, so you know whether it will blow up before you watch it blow up.

The output is a physics package that BlockForge imports and gives geometry and pictures, like a fireball whose path bends in a crosswind because the laws say so.

**Stack:** TypeScript, C++, CMake.
