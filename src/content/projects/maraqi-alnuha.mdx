Most learning platforms track completion: lessons watched, quizzes passed. Completion says very little about what someone can do. Maraqi al-Nuha is built around evidence instead. The domain model is a ledger of what a learner has shown they can do, organised as a path of skills, and progress means new evidence, not a ticked box.

The site is live and fully bilingual, with real right-to-left layout in Arabic rather than a mirrored afterthought, and bilingual SEO and Open Graph cards so links look right in both languages. The platform behind it is still in development.

**Stack:** HTML, JavaScript.
