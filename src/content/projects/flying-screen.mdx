I wanted a screen that floats next to me. Before building anything, I wanted to know whether physics even allows it. Given a screen, a mission and today's batteries and motors, does any feasible hovering aircraft exist, and if so, what is the best one?

Flying Screen is a co-design engine, a browser design tool and a paper that answer that. The model goes in layers, from momentum theory and mass closure up to Newton-Euler dynamics, control and optimisation, and every number traces back to an equation.

The finding I find most interesting is the feasibility wall. As the screen grows, the mass that has to lift the batteries that lift the mass stops converging. Past a fold point there is no equilibrium mass at all, so no amount of clever engineering helps. The tests check the engine's sensitivities against the closed-form theory, and MuJoCo simulations show the designs that do work flying their routes.

**Stack:** Python, NumPy, MuJoCo, LaTeX, HTML/JS.
