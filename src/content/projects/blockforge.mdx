BlockForge started as a logo for my brother's coding academy and turned into the biggest thing I have built. It is a world compiler: you write a world as source (build plans, a structure language, generators, Luau mods), and the engine compiles it into a voxel world, then animates and films it.

The problem I cared about is that 3D scenes are usually opaque files you cannot diff, merge or rebuild from a recipe. And agents are bad at working blind. So BlockForge gives them eyes: a `snapshot` call renders any view off-screen, and `query` tells them exactly what is in the world.

Two things I am proud of:

- **Seekable rendering.** Every frame is a pure function of story time, so a still, a scrub and a full render of the same moment give the same pixels, in the browser and in the native C++ client.
- **Git for worlds.** Every change becomes a version with its recipe attached, and branches diff block by block and merge three ways.

**Stack:** JavaScript, Three.js (WebGPU/WebGL), C++17, SDL2, Luau, WASM, MCP, Cloudflare Workers.
