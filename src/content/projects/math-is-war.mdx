Graphwar is an old Java game where you aim by typing a function, and its curve becomes your shot across the Cartesian plane. It is the most fun I have seen anyone have with functions, and it does not run anywhere people are today.

Math Is War is my browser-first reimagining in TypeScript. I rewrote the gameplay math with a new architecture, then added the part I think was missing: an academy, with courses and a level select, so a student can learn what a function does before using it to hit someone. It also ships as a Tauri desktop app and Android and iOS shells.

**Stack:** TypeScript, Tauri, Capacitor.
