Atlagene is a genomics startup with a consumer and a B2B side, which I co-founded with Yasser Shkeir. Yasser designed and built the platform itself: the consumer app, the lab portal and the analysis pipeline. My side is engineering and infrastructure, the accelerator programs and the work on the ground in Lebanon, and the product story: strategy, the pitch and how we show up at events.

The piece of that I built with my own hands is an interactive exhibition booth in Three.js, at true scale and walkable on desktop and touch, so people who never make it to an event can still visit. The first version took about 10 seconds to paint on emulated 4G. Splitting the payload so the scene paints first and the rest streams in brought it to about 2 seconds, which is the difference between someone staying and someone closing the tab.

**Stack:** Three.js, esbuild, Next.js, TypeScript.
