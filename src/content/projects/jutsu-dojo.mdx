This one started as a small applet on my H canvas and got out of hand. You make a sequence of hand seals in front of your webcam, and the right sequence casts an effect (water, fire, a fireball across an arena) into a Three.js scene.

The hard part is recognition. Generic hand-pose models are bad at telling similar seals apart, and everyone's hands are different. So recognition is personalised: you record a few samples of each seal, and a k-NN classifier learns your hands. A patch classifier plus sequence priors handle chains of seals, so a half-formed seal in the middle of a sequence does not derail the cast.

The simulation underneath is deterministic, with replay hash checks, so the same input always gives the same spell.

**Stack:** TypeScript, MediaPipe, Three.js, Express.
