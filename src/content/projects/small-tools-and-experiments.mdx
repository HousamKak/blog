Not everything needs to be a platform. These are the smaller things I built along the way, because I wanted them.

- **Wordle in English and Arabic**, as two separate games.
- **A crossword builder** that lays out a grid from a set of clues, with 2D and 3D graph views of how the words link.
- **Dama**, the draughts game, in the browser.
- **A maze game** with its own soundtrack.
- **Hand keyboard**, a generative model of typing motion that turns text into two-hand joint trajectories and simulated finger-worn sensor signals.
- **An OBS round mask**, a C++ plugin that masks a camera into a circle.
- **Telegram bots** that translate PDFs and play the card game Li5a against AI players.
- **DepeNsee**, a dependency visualiser for JavaScript and TypeScript projects.
