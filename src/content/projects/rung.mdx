Self-hosted servers rot. You tweak something over SSH, forget it, and six months later nobody can rebuild the box. Rung is my answer: the whole server is described in OpenTofu and cloud-init, so any Dockerized app can be deployed, destroyed and rebuilt without losing data.

The test suite is a drill. `make drill` destroys the box, recreates it, restores the data and times the whole thing. If the drill passes, I trust the setup. If it does not, I found out on my own schedule instead of during an outage.

The rule that makes this work is that an app cannot deploy until it declares how its state survives, using Turso, Litestream or wal-g depending on the data. There is no daemon or control panel on the box, nothing to babysit.

**Stack:** OpenTofu, cloud-init, Docker, Litestream, wal-g, Turso.
